How to Use the TradeRisk Calculator

Our risk calculator helps you manage your trades effectively. Follow these simple steps:

  1. Account Balance: Enter your total trading account balance. You can click on the currency (e.g., USD) to change it.
  2. Risk % per Trade: Decide what percentage of your account you are willing to risk on a single trade. Professional traders usually risk 1-2%.
  3. Stop Loss (pips): Determine your stop loss in pips for the trade you are planning to take.
  4. Pair: Select the currency pair you are trading from the dropdown menu.
  5. Calculate: Click the "Calculate" button to see your results instantly!

Why is Risk Management Important in Trading?

Risk management is the key to long-term success in the forex market. It's not about winning every trade; it's about making sure your losses are small and your wins are big enough to cover those losses and make a profit. By using a position size calculator like ours, you ensure that no single trade can wipe out your account. Proper risk management protects your capital, reduces emotional decision-making, and allows you to trade with confidence.
